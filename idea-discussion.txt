My data requirements decisions: Historical selection: 10 general + 10 home + 10 away. Statistics: First-15-minute score, Half-time score, Full-time score, First goal minute. Calculation sample: 10 matches. Let's refine the minimum data model.

Perfect. Now we have a clear data collection direction. Let's turn those decisions into a formal Minimum Data Model (MDM) for our Football Match Verification Engine.

One important principle: we will separate raw football data from calculated statistics. This allows us to recalculate everything whenever we improve our verification formulas without fetching historical data again.
1. Final historical data requirements

For every upcoming fixture, we will retrieve:
10

General

Last matches regardless of venue
10

Home

Last matches at home
10

Away

Last matches away

For each historical match, collect only:

    Match date.

    Home team.

    Away team.

    First-15-minute score.

    Half-time score.

    Full-time score.

    First goal minute.

We will apply these requirements to both teams participating in an upcoming fixture.

There is one optimization: the same historical match can appear in multiple categories. We store it once and associate it with the relevant team-history views.

5. Three important data rules

I want these rules embedded in the system from the beginning.

Rule 1 — Historical ordering

Always fetch the latest 10 completed matches before the upcoming fixture. Never include the upcoming match or any match played after it.

Rule 2 — Data completeness

If a historical match has no reliable first-15-minute score, we must flag it as incomplete rather than assume 0–0.

Rule 3 — Sample size

A 10-match sample is our initial model, not proof of a reliable probability. A rate of 80% from 10 matches has considerable uncertainty. The dashboard must show the actual number of observations alongside every percentage.
6. One remaining design decision

There is one thing I'd like us to settle before finalizing the schema.

When we say 10 general + 10 home + 10 away, should the system:

    Retrieve all three groups for each team, independently, giving up to 30 historical records per team before deduplication.

    Or retrieve 10 general matches and only the relevant venue-specific group for each team in a fixture?

My recommendation is the first option because it gives us more flexibility to compare general, home and away performance without fetching additional historical data later.

Once we agree on this, our minimum data model will be ready to translate into PostgreSQL tables and API response schemas.
